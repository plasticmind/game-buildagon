"Ooame" is offered under both CC BY 4.0 and CC0. Buildagon uses it under CC0.

"Aquaria" and "Heavenly Loop" are seamless loops. Each file repeats its loop
four times from the lossless original, with a 10-second fade at the end, so it
plays as one piece without the clicks that looping an MP3 in a browser causes.

All files were converted or re-encoded to MP3, with loudness normalized to
-20 LUFS so no track plays louder than the others.
